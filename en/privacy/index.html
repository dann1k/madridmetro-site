Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary

Andén collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app sends nothing to any server.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show the nearest station and, only during a trip you start, to follow the trip in the background as well. Your location is processed on the device only and is neither stored nor sent.

Motion & Fitness

During a trip the app reads the device's motion activity and barometer to tell when you get on and off the train underground. This is processed on the device only.

Data kept on the device

Recent stations, your settings and the current trip are stored on the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

Reminders before your stop are local notifications scheduled on the device; no remote notification service is used.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. Links to other websites (CRTM, OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia) open in your browser and follow their own policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
