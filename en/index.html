Support

Do I need the internet?

No. The network and timetable are built into the app.

How accurate are the times?

They come from the weekday timetable: running times, average wait and walking between platforms. There is no real-time data; check service changes on Metro de Madrid's official channels.

I found a mistake in the data

Write to us with the station, the line and what is wrong. We fix the data in the next version.

Why does it ask for location and motion?

To show the station nearest to you and, during a trip you start, to tell when you get on and off the train. Everything is processed on the device. The app works without these permissions too.
